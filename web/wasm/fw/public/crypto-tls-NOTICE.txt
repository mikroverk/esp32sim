C3/C6 Wi-Fi and TLS firmware
===========================

Files: c3-crypto-tls.bin, c3-crypto-tls-bootloader.bin, c3-crypto-tls-ptable.bin,
       c6-crypto-tls.bin, c6-crypto-tls-bootloader.bin, c6-crypto-tls-ptable.bin.

The fixture source is examples/crypto-tls/Probe/Probe.ino, MIT (repository LICENSE).
It uses Arduino-ESP32 3.3.11, its bundled ESP-IDF 5.5.5 libraries, and
esp-rv32/2601 GCC 14.2.0. examples/crypto-tls/build.sh rebuilds the firmware and
retains the linkable application objects and ELF. Arduino core/library sources:
https://github.com/espressif/arduino-esp32/tree/3.3.11
ESP-IDF sources and pinned submodules:
https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/tree/v5.5.5

Linked components and licences
------------------------------
The application link maps were inspected for retained, nonzero loadable sections.
The archive inventory is in docs/evidence/c3-c6-sha-dma-wifi-cal/inputs.json.
This list describes the linked subset, not every library available in ESP-IDF.

* Arduino core, WiFi and Network: Arduino Team and Espressif contributors,
  LGPL-2.1-or-later. Full licence in crypto-tls-runtime-licenses.txt.
* ESP-IDF, Espressif Wi-Fi/PHY/coexistence/controller code, ESP-NOW,
  network-provisioning and diagnostics: Espressif Systems, Apache-2.0.
* Mbed TLS: ARM Limited and contributors, Apache-2.0.
* FreeRTOS: Amazon.com and contributors, MIT.
* lwIP: Swedish Institute of Computer Science and contributors, BSD-3-Clause.
* wpa_supplicant: Jouni Malinen and contributors, BSD.
* TLSF allocator: Matthew Conte, BSD-3-Clause.
* cJSON: Dave Gamble and cJSON contributors, MIT; full notice/licence in
  crypto-tls-runtime-licenses.txt.
* newlib C/math runtime: its per-file copyright holders and licences.
* GCC libgcc and libstdc++ runtime: Free Software Foundation and contributors,
  GPL-3.0 with GCC Runtime Library Exception 3.1. Both texts are included in
  crypto-tls-runtime-licenses.txt.

The FreeRTOS, lwIP, wpa_supplicant, TLSF, newlib and Apache-2.0 licence texts and
copyright notices are already distributed in wifi-station-NOTICE.txt, starting
at its individually labelled licence sections. That file's general ESP-IDF
inventory is not the linked-component list for this fixture.

No Mbed TLS test certificates, private keys, external firmware or downloaded
application code is included. The PSK and deterministic RNG are public test
inputs, never production credentials. The bootloaders are those shipped in the
pinned Arduino package; partition tables are generated from its default recipe.
